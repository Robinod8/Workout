Strength & Stability Circuit

Complements pickleball (2x/wk) and golf (1x/wk). Equipment: exercise ball, 2x 7lb dumbbells, yoga mat, medium resistance band.
4x per week 3 sets per move ~25–30 min Rest 30–45 sec

1 Band Pull-Aparts shoulders / rotator cuff

Hold band at chest height, arms extended. Pull apart until it touches your chest, squeezing shoulder blades together.

2 Ball Plank w/ Shin Roll-Ins core / anti-rotation

Forearms or hands on mat, shins on ball, plank position. Roll ball toward chest by pulling knees in, keeping hips level.

3 Dumbbell Goblet Squats legs / glutes

Hold dumbbell(s) vertically at chest, squat down, chest up, elbows brushing inside knees.

4 Ball Hamstring Bridge Curls posterior chain / balance

Lie on mat, heels on ball, lift hips to bridge, curl ball toward glutes, extend back out.
